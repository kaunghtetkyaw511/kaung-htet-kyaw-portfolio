The studio

We believe the most memorable interiors are not the loudest. They are the ones that make daily life feel natural.

Founded in 2014, North & Form is a collaborative studio working across architecture, interiors, furniture, and art direction.

Our process balances clarity with curiosity, bringing clients, craftspeople, and specialists into one considered conversation.
